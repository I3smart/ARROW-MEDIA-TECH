Common questions

What do I need for a remote session?

A PC connected to your LED controller with internet access, and the controller software installed. We tell you how to join the session when we confirm the time.

Which brands and screens do you support?

Novastar, Huidu and Kystar controllers, receiving cards and video processors, on indoor and outdoor LED screens of any pixel pitch.

Can you help if I bought the hardware elsewhere?

Yes. Remote support is open to any screen running the brands above, whether or not the parts came from us.

How do I buy a controller, card or power supply?

Use the Enquire / Buy via WhatsApp button on any item. Share your screen size and module type and we confirm the right part and the price.

Which power supplies do you stock?

5V 60A and 5V 40A SMPS from Wrong, Genergy and YY. Tell us your module power draw and we help you size the quantity.
